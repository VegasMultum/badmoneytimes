Robert Menendez (“Gold Bar Bob”) — Federal Bribery & Foreign Influence Case File

According to federal prosecutors and court filings, this case alleges a long-running bribery and foreign influence scheme involving a sitting U.S. Senator, intermediaries connected to foreign officials, and private actors seeking official favors. Prosecutors alleged the conduct involved the abuse of public office in matters touching foreign military aid, U.S. government information, business protection, and interference with criminal investigations.
